声笺 Personal

声笺是一个个人使用的语音备忘录应用。它在本地设备上录音、转写和整理笔记，并可以把用户选择的文件保存到用户自己的 Google 云端硬盘。

Shengjian (声笺) is a personal voice-memo app. It records, transcribes and organizes notes on the user's own devices, and can save files the user chooses to the user's own Google Drive.
